The next experiment should test how efficiently one predictor learns from available histories, while keeping the assignment’s MovieLens100K task intact. A separate richer-data experiment can test information we currently lack. Neither promises novelty or a breakthrough.

The completed evidence narrows the question:

- Increasing disjoint donor histories from 88 to 353 raised mean development nDCG@10 from 0.200030 to 0.237810, **18.89% relative**, with query histories fixed. This is a controlled donor-count comparison, **not an improvement over the full-data baseline**. Estimation and item coverage both changed; the range cannot establish a ceiling or justify extrapolation. [Donor curve](donor-curve-v1/RESULTS.md)
- Exact independently weighted pair features produced 0.261660 versus binary 0.261688; allowing binary fallback yielded 0.261735. This is essentially a null result for the tested capacity increase, not a proof that all context modeling fails. [Interaction results](../context_interactions/results-v1/RESULTS.md)
- Binary top-10 lists allocate about 98.95% of slots to the head. Each seed has 1,525–1,620 held-out tail positives, but only 4–9 held-out positives have zero TRAIN support. Availability is therefore not enough: observing an item somewhere does not supply reliable evidence about who should receive it. Category-aware predictions overlap binary top-10 lists by 97.81%. [Error diagnosis](results-v1/RESULTS.md)
- The primary objective treats every recorded rating as relevant, including low ratings. Better record completion is not necessarily better enjoyment; liked-rating results must remain a separate endpoint.

**Assignment-preserving experiment: repeated evidence reconstruction.** Build one standalone denoising predictor with a 512-unit hidden layer and full-catalog output, avoiding the earlier field’s eight-dimensional state restriction. Repeatedly hide TRAIN observations and predict those records from the remaining history. This creates learning episodes, not new independent information. No validation interaction becomes training context.

Use a predeclared 2×2 comparison: fixed versus resampled context masks, and squared reconstruction versus multinomial record likelihood. Keep architecture, initializations, update budgets, candidate masks and tuning opportunities matched; include the locked EASE control. For both losses, supervise only positions absent from context; missing records are competing outcomes, not confirmed dislikes. Average losses per user. This tests whether objective alignment and repeated use of existing evidence help more than extra pair capacity.

Select checkpoints on meta-fit all-observed nDCG@10, seal every seed before development evaluation, and report all arms, runtime, MRR, recall and head/tail denominators. Preserve the declared primary endpoint even if another metric looks better. Previously examined TEST and reused development cannot become fresh confirmation; a separate untouched population is needed for a stronger claim.

**Information-acquisition extension.** Compare passive prediction with a policy choosing which feedback to request, against random acquisition using identical answer budgets and predictor capacity. Hide answers until queried; reserve separate unqueried outcomes for evaluation. Missing logged answers cannot be fabricated as negative feedback, and offline query simulation cannot establish live satisfaction gains.
