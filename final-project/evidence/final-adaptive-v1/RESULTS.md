# final-adaptive-v1: frozen held-out evidence

`aggregates.json` and `manifest.json` are exact copies of the completed evaluation artifacts.
All preselected models are reported. No checkpoint, hyperparameter or model was changed using TEST.
Manifest entries for excluded per-user files preserve provenance; those files are not included here.

Conditional rating likelihood and the P(rating ≥ 4) ranking diagnostic are distinct objectives.
